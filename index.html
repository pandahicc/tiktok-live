Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ATK.

What is ATK — TikTok Live Monitor?

ATK is a Chrome extension that provides real-time analytics for TikTok live streams. It tracks chat messages (danmu), virtual gifts, viewer events, likes, followers, and engagement metrics. It includes keyword-based alerting with webhook support for Slack, Discord, Lark, and Telegram, sentiment analysis (PRO), dynamic word clouds, and one-click Excel export.

Is ATK free to use?

Yes. ATK offers a generous free tier with real-time monitoring, up to 5 alert rules, 30-record export limit, Top 50 word cloud, 20-minute chart windows, and a Top 10 leaderboard (user info blurred). Upgrade to PRO ($9.9/month or $69/year) for unlimited exports, full charts, sentiment analysis, and advanced features.

Does ATK work with all TikTok live streams?

Yes. ATK works on any public TikTok live stream. Simply navigate to any live room on tiktok.com and click the ATK icon in your Chrome toolbar. The extension automatically detects the live stream and begins data collection.

How do keyword alerts and webhooks work?

You define alert rules with specific keywords (e.g., "refund", "shipping delay", "counterfeit"). When a chat message matches a keyword, ATK triggers a desktop notification and optionally highlights the message on the page. You can also configure webhooks to push alerts to Slack, Discord, Lark, or Telegram in real time. Built-in e-commerce presets are available for quick setup — product quality, service complaints, price disputes, logistics, and live content monitoring.

Is my data private and secure?

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. No data is sent to external servers except the webhook URLs you explicitly configure. ATK does not access your TikTok credentials, and you can clear all stored data at any time from the extension settings.

Can I use PRO on multiple devices?

Yes. Your PRO subscription is linked to your license token. Activate it on any Chrome browser by entering your token in the extension settings. Use it across all your devices with a single subscription.

How do I get started?

Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store, open any TikTok live stream, and click the ATK icon in your toolbar. The analytics dashboard opens automatically. No sign-up required for the free tier. For PRO, click "Subscribe" in the extension and complete payment via Stripe.
